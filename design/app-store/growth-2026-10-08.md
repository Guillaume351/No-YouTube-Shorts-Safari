## Capture and claim limits

- Avoid personal accounts, avatars, notifications and debug UI.
- Preserve Safari chrome so website scope is visible.
- Show only settings and behavior verified in the submitted binary.
- The local activity signal proves that the extension executed recently; it
  cannot prove that every Shorts element was removed.
- Do not claim saved minutes, a guaranteed block inside the native YouTube app,
  YouTube Premium, or tamper-proof parental controls.
- BrowseKind is separate; the three-extension bundle excludes BrowseKind Plus.
- The existing website already explains scope and purchase model; this draft
  does not authorize or create a website change.
