### Description (1235/4000)

```text
Hide YouTube Shorts in Safari. Keep the videos you chose.

No Shorts is a Safari extension that removes the Shorts shelf, the Shorts tab and Shorts in search results on youtube.com, so you can watch long-form videos without falling into the endless scroll.

WHAT IT HIDES
• Shorts shelves on Home, Subscriptions, channels and search
• The Shorts tab and sidebar entry
• The Shorts filter in search results

OPTIONAL CONTROLS
• Hide the Home feed: Home stays blank so you decide where to go next
• Hide video recommendations around the player (playlists and live chat stay)
• Hide comments under videos
Change any of them from the extension button in Safari. Changes apply right away.

SIMPLE SETUP
Turn it on in Safari settings and allow it on youtube.com. The app checks that everything works and tells you when No Shorts is active on YouTube.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Everything runs on your device
• No account, no tracking, no data collection
• Only runs on youtube.com

PAY ONCE
One purchase, no subscription. Works on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Vision Pro.

Important: No Shorts works on YouTube in Safari. It does not change the YouTube app.

Also available: No Reel for Instagram and No Algorithm for X, sold together as a bundle.
```
