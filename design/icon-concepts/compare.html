Read on each

Current

Reads as a video player or a “C” logo. The sparkle suggests AI. Nothing says “block”.

A · Slashed Short

Most distinctive: the only red tile in the row. The vertical card says “short-form video”, but the slash over a white card is a little busy at 40pt and below.

B · No-sign, dark

Clearest meaning at every size: a universal “no” sign over a play button. The dark tile stands out on the white App Store background and looks the same in dark mode.

C · No-sign, light

Closest to today's brand (light tile with a red ring), so it's the safest change. It blends in more on the white store background.

D · Teal card

ChatGPT's idea, made readable: one solid ink slash with no outline gaps, and a bigger play triangle. Calm and distinctive, but it can read as “no phone”.

E · Teal no-sign

C's “no” sign in white on teal: it keeps C's clarity and gains D's calm, distinctive colour. The red play has a little less contrast on teal.
